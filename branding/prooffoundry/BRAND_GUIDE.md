# PROOFFOUNDRY — concept

VERIFIED DISCOVERY ENGINE

**Tagline:** From conjecture to evidence.

**Visual direction:** An open proof lattice converging into a verified keystone; warm technical design with no generic flame or shield.

**Palette:** Carbon / copper amber; dark background `#141310`, accent `#F0B66B`, light accent `#874B12`.

Assets: `icon.svg` (square, scalable), `banner.svg` (5120×1920 intrinsic, vector), `tokens.json`, `tokens.css`.

Use the icon at small scale, the banner at its native aspect ratio, system typography and neutral panels. Keep the status colors independent from the brand. Dark theme default; light theme equally supported. Never rely on color alone to distinguish proof/finite test/failure. Add keyboard focus and reduced-motion preference to production UI.

Name is provisional, not trademark-cleared.
